Who signs NEAR's transactions?

Function-call keys are a few percent of new keys, but they sign 13.1% of transactions. 134 of those 200 use a key for intents.near, from 1 signer; in all, 29 accounts sign this way against 384 with full-access keys. Source: node thesis/txshare.mjs, 300 random blocks from 2026-10-01 to 2026-11-13 (seed 1001), 1,521 transactions, each signing key read on chain.

What this means for a NEAR holder

Fees burnt offset about 1% of new issuance, so supply still grows about 2.47% a year. Burn would have to grow about 92 times to cancel issuance, so agent-safe keys are not yet a supply effect. This is the status of a thesis, not investment advice.
